Tamworth Castle
The castle is a Norman castle. It occupies a site on which a Saxon castle stood. Tamworth was a centre of the Mercian kingdom. The castle's development and features are described on the Historic England website at Link (Archive Link ) and in The Buildings of England Staffordshire by Christopher Wakeling and Nicholas Pevsner, Yale University Press, ISBN 978-0-300-21835-0. The castle is grade I listed.
