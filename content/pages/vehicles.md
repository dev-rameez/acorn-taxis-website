# Vehicles

**H1:** Our vehicles  

**Intro:**  
With a fleet of **over 100 vehicles**, Acorn Taxis can match the right car to your journey. Tell the operator what you need when you book.

---

## Vehicle types

### Saloon
Comfortable standard cars for everyday local journeys and shorter trips.

### Estate
Extra boot space when you are travelling with more luggage.

### 5–8 seater
Larger vehicles for families, small groups and trips where more seats are needed.

---

## When you book

Let us know:

- How many passengers are travelling  
- How much luggage you have  
- Whether you need a larger vehicle  

We will send a suitable vehicle from our fleet.

**Important:** Do **not** mention wheelchair-accessible vehicles on this page (client instruction).
