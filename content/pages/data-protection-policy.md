**H1:** Data Protection Policy  

This Policy sets out our obligations regarding data protection and the rights of business contacts, customers and prospective customers (“data subjects”) in respect of their personal data under applicable UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Acorn Taxis Limited is committed to the lawful, fair and transparent handling of personal data, respecting the privacy and trust of everyone we deal with.

## Data protection principles

Personal data must be:

1. Processed lawfully, fairly and transparently  
2. Collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes  
3. Adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary  
4. Accurate and kept up to date  
5. Kept no longer than necessary  
6. Processed securely, using appropriate technical and organisational measures  

---

## Rights of data subjects

Data subjects have rights including:

- To be informed  
- Of access  
- To rectification  
- To erasure  
- To restrict processing  
- To data portability  
- To object  
- In relation to automated decision-making and profiling  

Details of how to exercise these rights are also set out in our [Privacy Notice](/privacy-notice/).

---

## Lawful processing

We only process personal data where a lawful basis applies, such as contract, consent, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, or legitimate interests (balanced against individuals’ rights).

Special category data is only processed where an additional lawful condition applies.

---

## Purposes and retention

We collect and process personal data for delivering taxi and private hire services, operating accounts, communicating with customers and contacts, complying with legal duties, and improving our operations where lawful.

Retention follows business and legal need. Typical website-facing periods (also in the Privacy Notice):

- Enquiries without engagement: about 3 months from last communication  
- Customers who engage services: about 7 years from last communication  

---

## Security and sharing

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage.

Personal data may be shared with contractors who help us deliver services, and where disclosure is required by law.
