Help

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a taxi?

Book online, use the Acorn Taxis app, or call 01827 63333. Private hire journeys must be pre-booked.

Are you open overnight?

Yes. We are open 24/7.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. Pay by cash, by card in the app, or by card in the vehicle. Mention card payment when you book if you need it in the vehicle.

Do you go to the airport?

Yes — including Birmingham, Manchester, Heathrow, Luton and East Midlands. Pre-book your transfer.

Can I book by email?

No. Please book online, through the app, or by phone. Use the contact form for general enquiries only.
