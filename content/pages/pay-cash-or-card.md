# Pay Cash or Card

**H1:** Pay your way  

**Intro:**  
Acorn Taxis gives you flexible payment options so you can settle your fare in the way that suits you.

---

## Payment options

### Cash
Pay your driver in cash at the end of the journey.

### Card in the app
Pay by card when you book through the Acorn Taxis app.

### Card in the vehicle
Pay by card with your driver. Contactless payments are supported. Let the operator know at the time of booking if you need a vehicle that can take card payments.

---

## Tip

If you are booking from outside the Tamworth area through the online booker, upfront card payment may be required — follow the prompts in the booking service.
