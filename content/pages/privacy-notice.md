## 2. What does this notice cover?

This Privacy Notice explains how we use your personal data: how it is collected, how it is held, and how it is processed. It also explains your rights under the law relating to your personal data.

---

## 3. What is personal data?

Personal data is any information about you that enables you to be identified. It covers obvious information such as your name and contact details, and less obvious information such as identification numbers, electronic location data, and other online identifiers.

---

## 4. Your rights

Under data protection law you have rights including:

- The right to be informed about our collection and use of your personal data  
- The right to access the personal data we hold about you  
- The right to have your personal data rectified if it is inaccurate or incomplete  
- The right to ask us to delete or otherwise dispose of your personal data  
- The right to restrict processing of your personal data  
- The right to object to us using your personal data for a particular purpose  
- Rights relating to data portability and automated decision-making / profiling where applicable  

For more information, contact us using the details above. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) or your local Citizens Advice Bureau. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO.

---

## 5. What personal data do we collect?

We may collect personal data needed to take bookings, provide transport, operate accounts, and respond to enquiries. If you do not allow us to collect necessary data, we may not be able to fulfil our contractual or legal requirements.

---

## 6. How do we use personal data?

We always need a lawful basis, such as:

- Performance of a contract with you  
- Your consent  
- A legal requirement  

Purposes may include providing and managing your account, supplying our services, communicating with you, and (only where permitted) sending information you have opted in to receive. You can opt out of marketing at any time.

---

## 7. How long do we keep personal data?

- Enquiry only (no engagement): typically **3 months** from the last communication  
- Where you engage our services: typically **7 years** from the last communication  

We will not keep personal data longer than necessary for the reason it was collected.

---

## 8. Where do we store personal data?

We aim to store and transfer personal data with appropriate protections under UK data protection law. Ask the data protection lead for current processing location details if you need them.

---

## 9. Do we share personal data?

We may share personal data with contractors working on our behalf where needed to deliver the service, and where we are legally required to do so (for example court orders or regulatory instructions).

---

## 10. Accessing your personal data

You can make a subject access request in writing to the email or postal address above. We will normally respond within one month. In more complex cases more time may be required (up to three months). You will be kept informed.

---

## 11. Contact

To contact us about privacy or data protection, including subject access requests, use the details in section 1.
