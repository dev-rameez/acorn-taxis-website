## Our approach

While specific emissions figures and formal certification details are still to be confirmed with the business, our day-to-day approach includes:

- Maintaining a modern, regularly serviced fleet of **over 100 vehicles**  
- Using professional dispatch technology to match journeys efficiently  
- Encouraging app and online booking to reduce unnecessary phone waiting and repeat calls  
- Supporting shared and group travel with larger 5–8 seater vehicles where appropriate
